FrontDesk AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

FrontDesk AI is a WhatsApp-based virtual receptionist designed to help businesses manage customer inquiries and appointment bookings.

Information We Collect

When you communicate with FrontDesk AI, we may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your WhatsApp profile name
  • Messages you send to the service
  • Appointment information such as requested service, date, and time

How We Use Information

Information is used only to process customer requests, answer inquiries, create or manage appointments, and provide automated receptionist services.

Data Sharing

FrontDesk AI does not sell personal information. Data is processed only as necessary to provide the service.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide appointment and customer service functionality or as required by applicable law.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their information at any time. Instructions are available on our Data Deletion page.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion requests, contact the FrontDesk AI administrator using the contact email listed for the application.
