FrontDesk AI - Data Deletion Instructions

Users of FrontDesk AI may request deletion of their personal information.

How to Request Deletion

Send a data deletion request to the contact email associated with the FrontDesk AI application.

Please include:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • A statement requesting deletion of your FrontDesk AI data

After the request is verified, associated customer and appointment information will be deleted unless retention is required by law.

Data That May Be Deleted

  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Customer name
  • Stored messages
  • Appointment information
